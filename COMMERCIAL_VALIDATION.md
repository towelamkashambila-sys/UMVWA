# UMVWA Commercial Validation Gate

Technical completion is not commercial proof.

## Required external evidence
1. Interview 5-10 Executive/EA pairs about real coordination failures.
2. Observe existing meeting-prep/delegation workflows.
3. Pilot UMVWA with 3-5 pairs.
4. Measure time-to-understanding, missed dependencies, clarification cycles, carry-forward, repeated weekly use, and user-reported alignment.
5. Test willingness to pay with a real pricing conversation or paid pilot.
6. Compare against the user's existing combination of email + chat + calendar + task manager.
7. Record objections and product failures as canonical research evidence.

## Exit condition
Do not claim product-market fit until users repeatedly use UMVWA for a painful recurring workflow and at least some users demonstrate willingness to pay.
